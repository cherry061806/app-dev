# The Love Hypothesis

*A romantic comedy film based on the novel by Ali Hazelwood*

---

## A. Introduction
**The Love Hypothesis** is a 2026 romantic comedy film adapted from Ali Hazelwood's 2021 bestselling novel. It follows a PhD student who fakes a relationship with a well-known professor, only to find that the experiment starts to feel real.

> What begins as a fake relationship might just turn into something real.

---

## B. Main Characters

### Main Leads
- **Olive Smith** – a PhD candidate (played by Lili Reinhart)
- **Adam Carlsen** – a respected and intimidating professor (played by Tom Bateman)

### Supporting Character
- **Anh** – Olive's best friend, whose white lie starts the whole story

---

## C. Plot Summary
1. Olive tells a white lie to her best friend, claiming she is dating someone.
2. To make the lie believable, she kisses Adam Carlsen, a professor she barely knows.
3. Olive and Adam agree to fake date, since each of them benefits from it.
4. As they spend more time together, their pretend relationship starts to become real.
